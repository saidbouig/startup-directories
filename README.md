# Startup directories, checked

228 startup and launch directories with a working submit page, and what their **free** route
actually gives. Maintained by [LaunchGrill](https://launchgrill.com/directories?src=github), which submits its own product to them
and writes down what happened.

- **`link` is measured, not copied.** It is what a live free listing's HTML carries — `dofollow`, `nofollow`,
  `mixed`, `none` or `paid-only` — or `unknown` when nobody has measured it yet. 83 of 228 are measured.
  Directory marketing pages are not a source.
- **`checked` is a real date**, and `checkedBy` says how: `form walked` and `submit page read` are a person;
  `scanned by script` is not.
- 217 directories have a full page on launchgrill.com, checked by a person within the last 60 days.
  See also [the report on 20 "free" directories](https://launchgrill.com/directories/report?src=github).
